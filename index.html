The Academy Concept & Philosophy

Artistry Rooted in Mindfulness & Heritage

Nestled in the historic grandeur of Château d'Acoz in Gerpinnes, Belgium, the César Franck Summer Academy is a highly selective masterclass series dedicated to nurturing exceptional classical talent. Our core philosophy bridges rigorous technical mastery with deep psychological alignment.

We believe true artistry requires more than hours at the instrument. By cultivating inner quietude, breath awareness, and absolute stage presence in an authentic historical sanctuary, young musicians learn to transform performance anxiety into profound, transcendent communication with their audiences.

Every element of our program—from daily masterclass coaching by internationally celebrated faculty to evening salon recitals and contemplative walks through the moated castle grounds—is curated to awaken the highest tier of creative expression.

Musical Rigor

World-class coaching, individual instruction, concerto immersion, and profound technique refinement.

Mind & Spirit

Workshops focusing on performance psychology, stage anxiety release, and holistic artist presence.

Governance & Artistry

Leadership Team

President

Tony Cammaert

Owner and meticulous restorer of the historic Château d'Acoz, Tony Cammaert provides foundational governance and visionary stewardship for the academy. His profound dedication to architectural preservation, spatial harmony, and the timeless beauty of the estate creates a peaceful, inspiring sanctuary that shapes every participant's experience.

Furthermore, his lifelong passion for fine arts and East-West philosophical dialogue infuses the academy with an atmosphere of quiet reflection and deep creative exploration. Under his stewardship, the historic halls of Château d'Acoz become a living bridge between historical heritage and contemporary artistic excellence.

Artistic Director

Philippe Raskin

An internationally acclaimed concert pianist and pedagogue, Philippe Raskin curates the academy's uncompromising artistic vision, masterclass standards, and world-class faculty. His extensive international concertizing career on global stages brings rigorous conservatory standards directly to the students.

Dedicated deeply to mentorship, Maestro Raskin's teaching philosophy emphasizes tonal color, structural architectural awareness in piano and chamber repertoire, and psychological resilience. His leadership ensures that every active participant receives world-class preparation for international stages.

Executive Director

Cristiana Dragos

An award-winning pianist, faculty mentor, and seasoned cultural project manager, Cristiana Dragos oversees the strategic execution, daily masterclass scheduling, and administrative leadership of the academy, ensuring seamless operations throughout the session.

Her expertise in international arts administration and student coordination guarantees an uplifting, supportive environment where young musicians can focus entirely on their craft. She acts as a vital bridge between faculty, administrative partners, and participating artists.

General Manager

Anne Noel

Anne Noel oversees on-site operational logistics, participant housing at Domaine du Château de Lausprelle, and daily event coordination. Her organizational care ensures that students and faculty experience absolute comfort and hospitality during their stay.

With extensive experience in hospitality management and cultural event staging, she coordinates transportation, shuttle timetables between gîtes and the Château, and catering services, allowing participants to immerse themselves fully in their musical studies without distraction.

Advisor

Meng Li

Bringing exceptional expertise in international cultural exchange, global educational outreach, and artistic partnership development, Meng Li serves as a strategic advisor to the César Franck Academy administration.

Her insights bridge European classical traditions with international conservatory networks, expanding the academy's global reach and fostering cross-cultural collaborations that enrich the artistic horizons of every student.

Location & Accommodation

Château d'Acoz & Lodging

All academy participants are accommodated in nearby comfortable gîtes at Domaine du Château de Lausprelle or with welcoming local Acoz host families to preserve a peaceful community environment.

Practice facilities and masterclass salons are organized in walking distance from the historic Château d'Acoz. Additionally, 4 daily shuttles will be organized between the accommodation gîtes and the masterclass location for effortless daily transit.
