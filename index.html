Academy Concept & Philosophy

Inspire. Perform. Grow.

Nestled within the tranquil moated estate of Château d'Acoz in Gerpinnes, Belgium, the César Franck Summer Academy is a premier international sanctuary dedicated to the holistic cultivation of classical musical talent.

Founded on the belief that profound artistic expression requires both technical mastery and mental equilibrium, our academy brings together world-renowned concert artists and pedagogues. Here, musicians transcend traditional practice routines by combining rigorous 1-on-1 instruction with cutting-edge performance psychology, stage anxiety management, and somatic posture alignment.

Musical Rigor

Intensive individual coaching, concerto soloist prep, and stylistic refinement under world-class conservatory faculty.

Mind & Spirit

Specialized mindfulness workshops focusing on performance psychology, stage presence, breathing control, and focus.

Heritage & Sanctuary

Immersion in the historic acoustic salons and serene manicured grounds of the historic Château d'Acoz estate.

Governance & Vision

Leadership Team

Guided by an esteemed international team uniting fine arts, performance mastery, and organizational excellence.

Tony Cammaert

President

As the owner and restorer of Château d'Acoz, Tony Cammaert leads the academy's overall governance and overarching strategic vision. His lifelong dedication to fine arts, architectural heritage restoration, and spatial harmony has transformed the historic estate into an inspiring sanctuary for classical artists.

Combining deep philosophical insights with East-West cultural dialogue, Tony also leads the academy's signature mindfulness and stage presence sessions. His holistic approach helps musicians cultivate internal equilibrium, mental focus, and authentic artistic expression on stage.

Philippe Raskin

Artistic Director

An internationally acclaimed concert pianist and pedagogue, Philippe Raskin curates the academy's artistic direction, masterclass standards, and faculty selection. Having performed in prestigious concert halls worldwide and served on juries of international piano competitions, he brings uncompromised artistic standards to the academy.

Philippe's passionate dedication to mentorship ensures that every active participant receives tailored artistic guidance. His coaching focuses on technical precision, tonal color palette, and deep interpretive understanding of classical and romantic masterworks.

Cristiana Dragos

Executive Director

An award-winning pianist, faculty member, and seasoned cultural manager, Cristiana Dragos oversees strategic execution, student admissions, and administrative leadership. Her extensive background in international project management guarantees seamless coordination across all academy tracks.

Cristiana is committed to fostering a supportive, inspiring environment where young artists can flourish. She coordinates student relations, masterclass schedules, and performance scheduling to ensure every participant enjoys a transformative week.

Anne Noel

General Manager

Anne Noel manages all on-site operational logistics, guest hospitality, and event coordination across Château d'Acoz and partner lodging facilities. Her attention to detail creates a welcoming, serene atmosphere for faculty and students alike.

From supervising catering and rehearsal salon allocations to coordinating shuttle transfers and concert receptions, Anne's dedicated leadership ensures the daily operations flow effortlessly throughout the academy session.

Meng Li

Advisor

Bringing valuable international expertise in global cultural exchange and artistic development, Meng Li serves as a strategic advisor to the César Franck Summer Academy. Her vision supports global partnership opportunities and student recruitment.

Her strategic counsel helps bridge classical music traditions with modern artistic initiatives, enhancing the academy's international profile and expanding opportunities for participating students on global stages.

Location & Accommodation

Château d'Acoz & Lodging Facilities

All academy participants are comfortably accommodated in nearby walkable gîtes at Domaine du Château de Lausprelle or with welcoming local Acoz host families to preserve a peaceful community environment.

Practice Facilities in Walking Distance

Dedicated acoustic practice salons equipped with grand pianos are organized within short walking distance from the Château.

4 Scheduled Daily Shuttles

Four organized shuttle services run daily between accommodation sites, practice rooms, and the main masterclass location.

Heritage & Practice Environment

Protective acoustic and physical measures safeguard the historical heritage of Château d'Acoz. Participants enjoy seamless access to tuned grand pianos, peaceful park grounds, and quiet study areas throughout the 7-day session.
